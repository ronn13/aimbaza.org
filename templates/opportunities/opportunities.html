Opportunities

Grants, fellowships, computing resources, and collaboration calls open to MbazaNLP community members.

Computing Access

DFKI HPC Cluster

MbazaNLP members can apply for access to the German Research Centre for Artificial Intelligence (DFKI) High-Performance Computing cluster for NLP research and model training.

Google offers free TPU access to researchers working on open-source NLP projects. Academic and community applicants are eligible.

Grants & Funding

Lacuna Fund

Funds the creation of labelled training data for machine learning in the Global South, with a focus on underrepresented languages including African languages.

Visit Lacuna Fund →
Mozilla Technology Fund

Supports open-source projects and research that advance trustworthy AI and internet health, including low-resource language technology.

Learn more →
AI4D Africa Grants

The Artificial Intelligence for Development Africa initiative funds NLP and AI research in African contexts, with grants for researchers and community organisations.

GIZ Digital Transformation

GIZ Rwanda's Digital Transformation Center supports digital public goods and open-source community infrastructure in Rwanda.
