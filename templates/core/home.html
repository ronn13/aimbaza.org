About MbazaNLP

We are an open community building Natural Language Processing (NLP) tools for Kinyarwanda and other low-resource African languages.

Our work spans automatic speech recognition (ASR), text-to-speech (TTS), machine translation, and semantic chatbots — all open-source and freely available. We welcome researchers, engineers, linguists, students, and anyone passionate about African language technology.
